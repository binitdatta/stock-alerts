Privacy Policy

Stock Alerts / Stock Command Center, operated by Rollingstone Technologies LLC · Effective October 1, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Rollingstone Technologies LLC collects, uses, stores, and protects information associated with the Stock Alerts / Stock Command Center application, including information used to provide optional SMS stock-price notifications to explicitly opted-in recipients.

Stock Alerts / Stock Command Center is a private stock-market monitoring application operated by Rollingstone Technologies LLC. SMS notifications are provided only to recipients who have voluntarily consented to receive them.

Information We Collect

Depending on how the Stock Alerts service is used, we may collect the following information:

  • Mobile phone number provided by a recipient who voluntarily opts in to receive SMS stock-price alerts.
  • SMS consent information, including whether SMS notifications are enabled and records associated with the recipient's consent or withdrawal of consent.
  • Stock alert configuration, such as stock symbols and user-configured price-change conditions used to determine when an informational alert should be generated.
  • Message delivery records, which may include message send time, delivery status, and message identifiers returned by our SMS communications provider.

How We Use Information

Rollingstone Technologies LLC uses this information only for purposes related to operating and supporting the Stock Alerts / Stock Command Center service, including:

  • Sending informational stock-price SMS notifications that the recipient has requested.
  • Processing and honoring SMS opt-out requests, including STOP requests.
  • Providing assistance in response to HELP requests.
  • Confirming message delivery and troubleshooting failed or delayed messages.
  • Maintaining operational, consent, security, and audit records associated with the SMS notification service.

We do not use mobile phone numbers or SMS consent information for advertising or promotional marketing.

SMS Privacy and Non-Sharing

Mobile phone numbers, SMS opt-in information, and SMS consent information collected by Rollingstone Technologies LLC / Stock Alerts will not be sold, rented, or shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes.

Mobile information and SMS consent information are used only to provide, operate, maintain, and support the informational SMS services requested by the recipient.

No mobile information will be shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. Information sharing with service providers that support delivery of the requested SMS service, such as telecommunications providers, is limited to what is necessary to provide that service.

SMS Service Providers

Rollingstone Technologies LLC uses third-party communications infrastructure to deliver requested SMS notifications.

A recipient's mobile phone number and SMS message information may be transmitted to our SMS communications provider, Twilio Inc., and to telecommunications carriers solely as necessary to deliver, route, troubleshoot, and support the SMS messages requested by the recipient.

These operational disclosures are not made for third-party marketing or promotional purposes.

SMS Message Frequency and Charges

Message frequency varies depending on market activity and the stock-price alerts configured by the user. Multiple alerts may be generated during periods of significant market activity.

Message and data rates may apply.

SMS Consent

Recipients voluntarily opt in to receive Stock Alerts SMS notifications by providing their mobile phone number and actively selecting an unchecked consent checkbox agreeing to receive automated informational stock-price alerts from Rollingstone Technologies LLC / Stock Alerts.

SMS consent is optional and is not a condition of purchasing any goods or services.

Your Choices

  • Reply STOP to any Stock Alerts SMS message to unsubscribe from future SMS notifications.
  • Reply HELP for assistance.
  • Recipients may also disable SMS notifications through the application's alert settings when that option is available.
  • Contact us to request access to, correction of, or deletion of information associated with the Stock Alerts service.

Retention and Security

Information associated with Stock Alerts is retained only for as long as reasonably necessary to operate the service, maintain appropriate operational and consent records, comply with applicable requirements, and resolve technical or security issues.

Rollingstone Technologies LLC uses reasonable administrative and technical safeguards intended to protect information from unauthorized access, disclosure, alteration, or destruction.

Informational Stock Alerts

Stock Alerts / Stock Command Center provides informational market-data notifications. SMS messages generated by the application do not constitute investment advice, financial advice, trading advice, or a recommendation to buy, sell, or hold any security.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

Rollingstone Technologies LLC may update this Privacy Policy periodically. The effective date displayed at the top of this page identifies the current version.
