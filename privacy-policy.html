Privacy Policy

Stock Command Center, operated by Binit Datta (registered brand: Binit Datta) · Effective October 1, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains what information Stock Command Center collects for its SMS stock-alert service, how that information is used, and the choices you have. Stock Command Center is a personal, self-hosted stock monitoring application operated by Binit Datta. Its SMS alerts are sent only to the account owner's own mobile phone.

Information we collect

  • Mobile phone number, entered by the account owner on the application's Alert Settings page to receive SMS alerts.
  • SMS consent record: whether SMS alerts are enabled, and when that setting was changed.
  • Message delivery records: for each alert, the send time, delivery status and the message identifier returned by our SMS provider.

How we use information

  • To send the stock price alert text messages you have requested.
  • To confirm delivery, troubleshoot failed messages and honour opt-out (STOP) requests.
  • To keep an audit record of consent and settings changes.

We do not use your phone number for marketing, and we do not send messages other than the alerts you enabled.

Sharing

We do not sell or share your SMS opt-in data or personal information with third parties for marketing purposes.

Your phone number and message content are transmitted to our SMS delivery provider, Twilio Inc., and to mobile carriers solely to deliver the messages you requested. We may disclose information if required by law.

Retention and security

Information is stored in a private database accessible only to the account owner. Alert and delivery records are kept for as long as the service is in use and can be deleted on request.

Your choices

  • Reply STOP to any alert to stop receiving messages, or uncheck "SMS enabled" on the Alert Settings page.
  • Reply HELP for help.
  • Contact us to access or delete your information.
