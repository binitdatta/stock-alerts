SMS Terms

  1. Program description. Stock Command Center sends automated stock price alert text messages when a stock on the account owner's watchlist moves $2, $5 or $10 from its previous closing price. Each message states the stock symbol, current price, the day's change and the threshold crossed.
  2. Opt-in. You opt in by entering your mobile number on the application's Alert Settings page and checking "SMS enabled: I agree to receive automated stock alert text messages".

  5. Opt-out. Reply STOP to any message to cancel. You will receive one final confirmation and no further messages. You can also uncheck "SMS enabled" on the Alert Settings page. Reply START to resubscribe.
