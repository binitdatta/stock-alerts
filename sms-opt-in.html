SMS Opt-In

Stock Command Center, operated by Binit Datta (registered brand: Binit Datta)

This page is a public copy of the consent step in the Stock Command Center application, published so it can be reviewed without signing in. The real form is on the application's Alert Settings page, behind a login, and is used only by the account owner to receive alerts on his own phone. This copy does not collect or submit any information.

Step 1 · Alert Settings: SMS consent

US number in international format. Only the account owner's own number is entered.

By checking SMS enabled, you agree to receive automated stock price alert text messages from Binit Datta (Stock Command Center) at the number above. Message frequency varies with market activity. Message and data rates may apply. Reply STOP to opt out or HELP for help. Consent is not a condition of any purchase. See our Privacy Policy and Terms & Conditions.

The checkbox is unchecked by default; nothing is sent until the owner checks it and saves.

Step 2 · What the owner sees after saving

✓ Saved 2 change(s).

The Alert Settings page reloads with the confirmation above, the box shown as checked and the saved number shown masked (for example +1••••••0100). From then on, alerts are sent only when a watched stock crosses a $2, $5 or $10 move from its previous close, at most once per stock per threshold per day.

Opting out

  1. Reply STOP to any alert. One final confirmation is sent and no further messages follow.
  2. Or uncheck SMS enabled on the Alert Settings page and save.
  3. Reply HELP for help, or email binitdatta@gmail.com.
